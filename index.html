About Malecombo Trading Enterprise CC

Company Overview

Malecombo Trading Enterprise CC was registered as a Close Corporation on 03 May 2011. The company is a multi-disciplinary business entity that has progressively positioned itself in the local, provincial, and national trading landscape within the mining, construction, and civil works sectors.

As an equal opportunity, 100% Black-owned enterprise with full interest holding, we were established to address the overall needs of the mining industry, building and construction, and civil engineering — delivering quality, safety, and transformation across all our projects.

Our Vision

To design and implement modern infrastructure through data-driven construction in the Mining Industry and other sectors, becoming a recognised leader in mining civil and construction support services in South Africa.

Our Mission

To supply the best construction and civil engineering services to our clients — on time, within budget, safely, and to specification — while developing our people and empowering local communities.

Management Structure

Elias Malose Bambo — Managing Director

Mr. Bambo brings over ten years of hands-on operational experience gained at Anglo American Platinum's Mogalakwena Complex (Valterra). His experience spans surface drill and blast operations as well as concentrator plant operations, giving Malecombo a unique combination of mining production knowledge and construction execution capability.

Key Operational Expertise:

  • Surface Blast-Hole Drill Operations: Certified operator with experience in hard-rock platinum reef geology (Atlas Copco / Epiroc PV-351 Pit Viper & Smartrock monitoring).
  • Concentrator Plant Operations: Direct experience in unblocking process pipework, unchoking screeners, maintaining conveyor belt systems, and collecting process samples.
